# اَلْوُسْطَى فِي النَّحْوِ — Wusta (Intermediate)

Welcome to **Wusta fi an-Nahw** (*Al-Mutawassit*), the intermediate stage of our classical Arabic grammar curriculum. Building directly upon *Sughra fi an-Nahw*, this syllabus distills the core rulings of classical intermediate primers (*Hidayatun-Nahw* and *Qatr an-Nada*) into structured principles and Qur'anic applications.

---

## الدَّرْسُ الْأَوَّلُ: اَلْجُمْلَةُ الِاسْمِيَّةُ (Lesson 1: The Nominal Sentence)

A **nominal sentence** (اَلْجُمْلَةُ الِاسْمِيَّةُ) is a complete predicative statement composed of two primary pillars (*ruknān*):

1. **اَلْمُبْتَدَأُ (The Subject / Inchoative):** A noun in the nominative case (*marfūʿ*), stripped of explicit grammatical governors (*ʿawāmil lafẓiyyah*), about which a judgment is made.
2. **اَلْخَبَرُ (The Predicate / Enunciative):** The nominative part that completes the benefit of the statement (*tammama al-fāʾidah*) alongside the *Mubtadaʾ*.

### شُرُوطُ الِابْتِدَاءِ بِالنَّكِرَةِ (Conditions for Starting with an Indefinite Noun)

By default, the *Mubtadaʾ* must be definite (*maʿrifah*). An indefinite noun (*nakirah*) may serve as *Mubtadaʾ* only when it yields a valid communicative benefit (*ifādah*), most commonly in the following cases:

- **التَّخْصِيصُ (Specification):** When modified by an adjective (*ṣifah*) or annexed in a construct (*iḍāfah*) — e.g., وَلَعَبْدٌ مُؤْمِنٌ خَيْرٌ مِنْ مُشْرِكٍ.
- **التَّعْمِيمُ (Generalization):** When preceded by negation (*nafy*) or interrogation (*istifhām*) — e.g., مَا رَجُلٌ فِي الدَّارِ, أَإِلَٰهٌ مَعَ اللَّهِ.
- **التَّأْخِيرُ (Inversion):** When preceded by a prepositional or adverbial *Khabar* (*shibh jumlah*) — e.g., فِي قُلُوبِهِمْ مَرَضٌ.
